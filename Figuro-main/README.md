# 🌱 Figuro — AI Learning Coach

Figuro is an AI learning coach designed to help students organize their learning, understand topics, practice, and receive personalized support using their own study materials.

## 🚀 Day 1 — Building the Foundation

Day 1 focused on building the core foundation of Figuro and connecting the main learning system.

### 🛠️ What I Built

* 👤 Real user accounts with email and Google sign-in
* 🔒 Private user data and account isolation
* 📚 My Lessons workspace
* 📁 Subjects, folders, nested folders, and lessons
* 📈 Lesson progress and lesson management
* 📄 Support for different study materials, including notes, files, links, and images
* 🔗 Study materials connected to their correct lessons
* 🤖 AI connected to the student's own study materials
* 📖 Learn, Practice, and Solve connected to the selected lesson and its materials
* 🌐 My Materials Only / My Materials + Web

### 🧪 Testing

I tested:

* Account privacy and data isolation
* Separate user accounts
* AI access to uploaded study materials
* Lesson and material connections
* Learn, Practice, and Solve with lesson context

### 🎯 Day 1 Goal

The goal of Day 1 was to establish a working foundation for Figuro so students can have their own accounts, learning content, study materials, and AI assistance in one place.

**Day 1: ✅ Complete**
